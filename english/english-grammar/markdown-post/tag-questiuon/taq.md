# Tag Question Rules & Practice

###  Rules of Tag Question

#### 1. Sentence অপরিবর্তিত থাকবে

**প্রদত্ত sentence টি অপরিবর্তিত অবস্থায় বসবে + tag question বসবে + (?) চিহ্ন বসবে।**

**Examples:**

- He is a student of class nine, <u>**isn’t he</u>?**
- They are not visitors, **<u>are they</u>?**
- She cooks dinner, **<u>doesn't she</u>?**
- We play football, **<u>don't we</u>?**
- The boy ran in the sun, **<u>didn't he</u>?**

---

#### 2. Affirmative Sentence → Negative Tag
#### 3. Negative Sentence → Positive Tag

**Examples:**

- He is a doctor, **<u>isn’t he</u>?**
- Rita was not a good learner, **<u>was she</u>?**

---

#### 3. Subject এর Pronoun ব্যবহার করতে হবে

**সবসময় subject এর pronoun রূপ বসবে।**

**Note:**

- Male person → **he**
- Female person → **she**
- বস্তু → **it**
- Plural → **they**

---

#### 4. Imperative Sentence এ Subject হিসেবে **you** বসে।

---

#### 5. Auxiliary Verb আছে কিনা দেখতে হবে

Tag question গঠনের সময় প্রথমে দেখতে হবে বাক্যের মধ্যে auxiliary verb আছে কিনা।

---

#### 6. Auxiliary Verb থাকলে সেটিরই Tag হবে।

**Examples:**

- He is a doctor, **<u>isn’t he</u>?**
- They are not visitors, **<u>are they</u>?**

---

#### 7. Auxiliary Verb না থাকলে মূল verb এর উপর নির্ভর করে **do / does / did** বসবে। 

**Examples:**

- They look fantastic, **<u>don't they</u>?**
- She makes delicious dishes for us, **<u>doesn’t she</u>?**
- They played cricket, **<u>didn’t they</u>?**

**Note:**  
***Sentence affirmative হলে negative tag এবং negative হলে positive tag হবে।***

---

#### 8. মূল Verb দিয়ে বাক্য শুরু হলে Question tag হিসেবে **will you / won't you / would you / could you** বসে।

**Example:**

- Open the door, **won't you?**
- Don't disturb school going girl, **will you?**
- Learn English from englishforowls.com, **will you?**
---

#### 9. Let + (me / us / him / them / her / you) Question tag হিসেবে **will you** বসে।

**Example:**

- Let him go now, **<u>will you</u>?**
- Let me live in peace, **<u>will you</u>?**
- Let us make the event successful, **<u>will you</u>?**
---

#### 10. Let's দিয়ে বাক্য শুরু হলে Question tag হিসেবে **shall we** বসে।

**Examples:**

- Let’s go out for a walk, **shall we?**
- Let’s solve the problem, **shall we?**
- Let's help the needy, **shall we?**
---

#### 11. There বাক্যের subject হলে, taq question এর pronoun হিসেবে there ই বসবে। 

**Example:**

- There were many problems, **weren’t there?**
- There is a hospital in our village, **isn't there?**

---


#### 12. Subject যদি nothing / anything / something / everything etc. হয় তবে taq question এ pronoun হিসেবে **it** বসে। 

**Examples:**

- Everything was lost, **wasn’t it?**
- Nothing is certain, **is it?**

---

#### 13 Subject যদি everybody / somebody / anybody / nobody / anyone / someone / none / no one etc. হয় তবে taq question এ pronoun হিসেবে ***<span style="color: purple">they</span>*** বসে। 

**Examples:**

- Everybody loves flowers, **don’t they?**
- Somebody came here, **didn’t they?**
- None can help you, **can they?**
- Nobody can succeed in life without hard work, **can they?**
---

#### 14 Subject যদি all of us / some of us / every one of us / most of us etc. হয় তবে taq question এ pronoun হিসেবে ***<span style="color: purple">we</span>*** বসে।


**Example:**

- All of us attended the meeting, **didn’t we?**

---

#### 15 Subject যদি all of them / some of them / every one of them / most of them etc. হয় তবে taq question এ pronoun হিসেবে ***<span style="color: purple">they</span>*** বসে।

**Example:**

- All of them were stupid, **weren’t they?**

---

#### 16. Subject যদি all of you / some of you / every one of you / most of you etc. হয় তবে taq question এ pronoun হিসেবে ***<span style="color: purple">you</span>*** বসে।

**Example:**

- Some of you can speak well, **can't you?**

---

### 17. Negative অর্থ প্রকাশকারী শব্দ sentence এ থাকলে taq question করার সময় not বসে না।

**সাধারণত নিচের শব্দগুলো negative অর্থ প্রকাশ করে:**
hardly, barely, seldom, rarely, scarcely, few, little, less, none, nothing, never, nowhere, neither etc. 

**Examples:**

- I can hardly sing a song, **can I?**
- There was little water in the bottle, **was there?**
- None can live alone, **can they?**

**Note:** a few / a little negative অর্থ প্রকাশ করে না।
- He has a few friends, **doesn't he?**
- There was a little water in the bottle, **wasn't there?**

#### 18. Subject One হলে taq question এ pronoun হিসেবে  ***<span style="color: purple">One / he / they </span>***   বসানো যেতে পারে। তবে formal English এ one বসে।

- One should respect the superiors, **shouldn't one?**
- One can not take such risk anymore, **can they?**

#### 19. "I am" দিয়ে sentence শুরু হলে taq question হিসেবে ***<span style="color: purple">aren't I </span>*** বসে।

- I am a stupid boy, **aren't I?**

---

#### 20. Sentence এ have to / has to / had to / had better থাকলে taq question এ ***<span style="color: purple">didn't // did </span>*** বসে।

- I have to work hard, **don't I?**
- She has to study attentively, **doesn't she?**
- You had better leave the place now, **didn't you?**


---

#### Sentence এ ought to থাকলে taq question এ oughtn't / ought বসে।

- We ought to obey our parents, **oughtn't we?**
---

#### need এবং dare auxiliary verb হলে, taq question হিসেবে এগুলোই বসে।

- You needn't worry, **need you?**
- He dare not say that, **dare he?**
---

#### Exclamatory sentence এর taq question

**Examples:**

- What a lovely day it is, **isn't it?**
- How beautiful she is, **isn't she?**
---

#### “This / That”  এর pronoun হিসেবে → **it** বসে ; “These / Those” এর pronoun হিসেবে → **they**
- This is my pen, **isn't it?**
- Those are your books, **aren't they?**
---

#### Subject + as well as / together with / along with + noun/noun phrase থাকলে, মূল subject অনুযায়ী Taq question এর pronoun হবে।
- Rebeka, as well as his friends, was present, **wasn't she?**
- Jekov together with his brothers is coming, **isn't he?**
  
---

#### যদি subject 3rd person singular হয় এবং verb এর সাথে s/es থাকে তবে doesn't / does বসে। কিন্তু যদি verb এর সাথে s/es না থাকে তবে didn't / did বসে। 

- She reads the story book, **doesn't she?**
- He shut the door quickly, **didn't he?**
---
